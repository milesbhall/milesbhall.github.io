## Inspiration

SurgiSafe was inspired by the need to catch surgical site infections (SSIs) early,
and by growing concern about methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* (MRSA).
The goal was to improve patient safety with timely alerts about possible wound
infections.

## What it does

SurgiSafe is a wearable device that monitors a wound by measuring moisture,
temperature, and pH in real time. Its readings give patients and healthcare
providers early alerts that can help prevent serious infections.

## How it was built

The device is built on Arduino, with sensors for moisture, temperature, and pH and a
simple interface that displays the readings. The design aims to be comfortable and
practical enough to wear every day.

## Challenges

The main challenges were getting accurate sensor readings and designing a device that
was both functional and comfortable. Integrating the hardware took careful
troubleshooting and testing. Two of the four team members were hackathon beginners.

## Built with

Arduino · Adafruit · Autodesk Fusion 360

## What's next

The team planned to refine the prototype based on user feedback, test it in clinical
settings, improve its accuracy, and add data analytics and remote monitoring.
